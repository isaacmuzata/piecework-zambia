Privacy Policy — PieceWork Zambia

Last updated: October 2, 2026

PieceWork Zambia ("we", "our") is a quick-labor marketplace that connects people who post piece work with people looking for work in Zambia. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, phone number, and city, which you provide when you sign up.
  • Profile and job content: job postings you create, applications you submit, photos or avatars you upload, and messages you send through the app where messaging is available.
  • Location: with your permission, your device location (GPS) is used to show jobs near you and to let posters mark a job's location. You can turn location access off in your device settings; job search by city still works.
  • Referral and giveaway data: your invite code, who referred you, and your giveaway entries, used to run the referral program and prize draws.
  • Device and technical data: a device identifier stored in a cookie (used to enforce one account per device), IP address, and basic app diagnostics used for security and fraud prevention.

How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and your job postings and applications.
  • To match workers with nearby jobs and show relevant listings.
  • To run the referral program and giveaway draws, including contacting winners.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, including duplicate accounts.
  • To respond to your questions and support requests.

What we share

  • Public profile and listings: your display name, city, and job postings or applications are visible to other users of the app, so workers and posters can find each other.
  • Giveaway winners: a winner's first name, phone number, and city may be shown on the live-draw screen so prizes can be paid via mobile money.
  • Service providers: we use third-party infrastructure (hosting and database providers) to run the app. They process data only on our behalf.
  • Legal: we may disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety and rights of our users.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

Your choices

  • You can update your profile information in the app at any time.
  • You can turn off location access in your device settings.
  • To request a copy of your data or ask us to delete your account and data, contact us at info@pieceworkzambia.com.

Data security and retention

We use reasonable technical measures to protect your information. We keep your account data for as long as your account is active, and delete or anonymize it when you ask us to close your account, unless we must keep it for legal reasons.

Children

PieceWork Zambia is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version here with a new "last updated" date.

Contact us
