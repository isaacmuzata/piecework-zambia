Delete Your Account — 2911 Jobs

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This page is for members of 2911 Jobs (the app listed on Google Play as 2911 Jobs, package com.pieceworkzambia.app, a product of 2911 IT Services). It explains how to delete your account and the data connected to it, including if you no longer have the app installed.

Fastest way: delete inside the app

  1. Open 2911 Jobs and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile, then Edit Profile.
  3. Scroll to the Account section at the bottom and tap Delete my account permanently.
  4. Confirm with your password. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

You can also choose Deactivate my account in the same section if you only want a break: your profile and listings are hidden and sign-in stops, and you can reactivate with one tap when you sign in again. Deactivation is not deletion.

If you no longer have the app

Email us at info@pieceworkzambia.com with the subject line Delete my 2911 Jobs account and include the phone number you registered with. We will verify the number is yours and complete the deletion for you. We aim to complete web and email requests within 7 days and will confirm by email or text when it is done.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile, name, city, photos and verification selfie
  • Your job postings, applications, marketplace listings and storefront
  • Your messages and reviews connected to the account
  • Your phone number is freed, so you may register again later as a new account if you wish

What may be kept, and why

  • Some records may be kept where the law requires it, or for fraud prevention and security, and are kept unlinked from your deleted profile where possible.
  • If your account is recorded as a giveaway winner, the win record is kept so the draw stays truthful; contact us and support will handle that deletion by hand.
